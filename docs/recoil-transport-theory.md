# Mathematical formulation

The formulation is developed in its two physical steps:

1. [Recoil transport](e001-ballistic-return.md): host phase space and gravity, joint initial conditions, Itô drift and diffusion, energy accounting, boundary conditions, and survival probability.
2. [Residence and environmental exposure](e002-core-exposure.md): finite-region crossings, time-resolved occupation, species exposure, censoring, and ensemble observables.

The [problem statement](problem.md) defines the scientific scope. The [calculation handoff](calculation-handoff.md) and [input register](../formulation/inputs.json) specify what must be supplied before numerical work. No trajectory or cluster fit has been produced for this formulation.
