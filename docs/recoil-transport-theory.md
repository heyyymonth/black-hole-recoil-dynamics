# Mathematical formulation

The formulation is developed in its two physical steps:

1. [Recoil transport](step1-transport.md): host phase space and gravity, joint initial conditions, Itô drift and diffusion, energy accounting, boundary conditions, and survival probability.
2. [Residence and environmental exposure](step2-exposure.md): finite-region crossings, time-resolved occupation, species exposure, censoring, and ensemble observables.

The [problem statement](problem.md) defines the scientific scope. The [calculation handoff](calculation-handoff.md) and [input register](../formulation/inputs.json) specify what must be supplied before numerical work. The [collisionless reference calculation](collisionless-residence.md) evaluates diagnostic remnant paths and occupation. Collisional trajectory ensembles and a new cluster fit are not supplied.
