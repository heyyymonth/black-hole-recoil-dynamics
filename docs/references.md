# References

- H. C. Plummer (1911), *On the Problem of Distribution in Globular Star Clusters*, MNRAS 71, 460-470. [DOI: 10.1093/mnras/71.5.460](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/71.5.460). The standard potential family used in the benchmark. Bibliographic record checked; no claim of reproducing the original paper's observational analysis.
- T. Islam (2026), *Recoil kicks from binary black hole mergers in GWTC catalogs: implications for retention and hierarchical mergers*. [arXiv:2604.04546v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04546v2). Selected displacement, return and repeat-merger sections reviewed; relevant prior work, not an implemented or tested model in this repository.
- S. Torniamenti et al. (2024), *Hierarchical binary black hole mergers in globular clusters: mass function and evolution with redshift*. [arXiv:2401.14837v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14837v2). Abstract and selected methods/discussion reviewed for the limits of a static host approximation.
- SciPy documentation: [solve_ivp](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.solve_ivp.html). The independent trajectory checks use a different numerical formulation from the production energy quadrature. Exact dependency versions are recorded with the results.

Source review date: 18 October 2026. Equations, numerical tables and figures in this repository are derived or generated by the supplied implementation. No observed-event data are used.

## E003 references checked 19 October 2026

- N. W. C. Leigh et al., *On the rate of black hole binary mergers in galactic nuclei due to dynamical hardening*. [arXiv:1711.10494](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10494). Context for the distinction between an encounter opportunity and subsequent hardening toward merger; its merger-rate model is not implemented here.
- galpy v1.12.0, [Isotropic Plummer distribution function](https://docs.galpy.org/en/v1.12.0/reference/dfplummer.html). Versioned primary implementation documentation for the equilibrium distribution f(E) proportional to (-E)^(7/2). E003 derives and integrates the associated local speed distribution independently; galpy is not a dependency.
- Islam, [sections V.1.3-V.1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04546v2). Rechecked the apocenter criterion of five half-mass radii and the distinction between a ballistic crossing and frictional recentering. E003 adopts only an illustrative orbit-admission screen.
