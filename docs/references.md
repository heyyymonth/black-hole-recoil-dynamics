# Primary literature for recoil transport and environmental exposure

- **Rosenbluth, MacDonald & Judd (1957), _Fokker-Planck Equation for an Inverse-Square Force_.** [Physical Review](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.107.1). Collision integrals underlying drift and diffusion. Number-density and mass-density distribution conventions must be distinguished.
- **Tep, Fouvry & Pichon (2022), _Non-resonant relaxation of anisotropic globular clusters_.** [Paper and equations](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.03985) · [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stac1362). Gravitational local-scattering coefficients and anisotropic relaxation; applicability remains a physical question.
- **Webb et al. (2018), _The Evolution of Kicked Stellar-Mass Black Holes in Star Cluster Environments_.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09100) · [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx3024). Kicked-black-hole motion, limitations of simplified friction, and energy changes from encounters. Its findings do not calibrate an unspecified host.
- **Gieles & Zocchi (2015), _A family of lowered isothermal models_.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.02120) · [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stv1848). Consistent mass components, anisotropy, and truncation provide one possible host family. No fit is adopted here.

These sources establish ingredients and limitations for the [two-step formulation](recoil-transport-theory.md), without supplying new numerical or observational evidence.
