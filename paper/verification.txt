Completed calculations
One actual NGC 3201 joint posterior sample is reconstructed with pinned,
historically compatible model implementations. Sixteen mass species supply
one consistent potential, density and local distribution-function family.
Twenty local states evaluate anisotropic drift and diffusion per unit constant
Coulomb logarithm. The 60-solar-mass remnant is a diagnostic probe.
Original fit-run software hashes are unknown; exact historical fit reproduction
and new observational fitting are not claimed.

Numerical checks
- Mass, half-mass radius, force, integrated potential and virial balance pass
  the stated numerical tolerances.
- Independent velocity quadrature recovers all-species density and raw moments.
- Independent projection checks the luminous tracer, with the original
  projected posterior envelopes retained as descriptive comparisons.
- Analytic uniform-ball kernels, mass/time factors, anisotropic rotational
  covariance, tensor trace identity, PSD and quadrature refinement pass.
- A separate one-dimensional central integral verifies the host's diffusion
  trace. Exterior test velocities are explicitly outside the current API's
  validated domain, rather than silently accepted by insufficient quadrature.
- Eight independent numerical tests pass in the recorded host environment.

Scientific limits
Numerical convergence does not validate smooth diffusion from a few heavy
objects. The expected black-hole count, density and scattering fractions are
conditional model quantities, not observed counts or measured relaxation.
The formal outer truncation radius is not an adopted tidal escape boundary.
No physical impact-parameter range, calibrated discrete-encounter partition,
merger-conditioned launch law, trajectory ensemble or residence distribution
has been evaluated. The recorded local coefficients identify the physical
questions to resolve before that evolution can be interpreted.
