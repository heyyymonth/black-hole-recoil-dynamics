Formulation and observed-cluster manuscript verification
Revision 0.5.0-manuscript.1; 24 October 2026

Scope
The article combines the two-step theoretical formulation with the completed
six-cluster data analysis. Typesetting reuses verified tables and vector
figures. No host fit, friction calculation, remnant orbit or residence
experiment is executed by the build or its verification.

Required integrity checks
- analysis/cluster_data/verify.py validates the captured data revision,
  source-record and derived-table consistency, candidate host records,
  completed notebook outputs and audited file hashes.
- paper/data_assets.py --verify validates the source-bound table and figure
  assets without regenerating them.
- scripts/verify_foundation.py checks the current formulation and metadata.
- paper/build.py verifies the evidence, typesets the declared source assets,
  checks LaTeX/BibTeX diagnostics, and creates the portable source archive.
- paper/verify.py checks source/output hashes, exact archive contents,
  citations, two-column source format, US Letter pages, embedded vector
  fonts, and absence of raster figures or internal experiment identifiers.

Presentation review
The final repository PDF has 10 US Letter pages and 22 embedded vector fonts.
All ten pages were rendered and visually inspected: title/author/abstract,
three evidence tables, three figures, equations, captions and references are
legible, with no clipping, overlap or missing glyphs observed. LaTeX reports
zero overfull boxes or unresolved references; BibTeX reports zero entry
warnings. The allowed REVTeX style diagnostic is unchanged.
The saved manuscript also compiles successfully with the desktop editor's
built-in compiler. The existing source editor was kept open.

Independent numerical review
Original Gaia catalog and HST profile counts were independently recomputed,
along with weighted mass-radius correlations and effective sample sizes from
the original published chains. The common median-radius-span intersection
was recomputed from original HST radii and the declared catalog scales. These
checks support the recorded data results, not physical transport validity.

Scientific boundary
Counts, unit conversions and published posterior summaries are completed
data-analysis findings. Their implications for source compatibility and
parameter dependence do not establish calibrated transport coefficients or
predict remnant residence. Historical numerical reference experiments are
not evidence for the active formulation and are not rerun for this revision.
