Scientific limits
No new observational fit or exact historical fit reproduction is claimed.
The pilot is not a converged posterior interval. Counts and diffusion shares
are conditional model quantities, not observed BH counts or residence shares.
NGC 3201 is a source-flagged model-applicability concern. NGC 6397 uses only
the available standard-fit chain; its preferred no-BH alternative is missing.
Rotation and population complexity remain unmodeled for Omega Centauri.
Numerical acceptance does not validate smooth diffusion from a few objects,
a stationary scattering reservoir, or the adopted Galactic potential.
The external-force bound is sufficient and instantaneous, not an escape
surface or accumulated trajectory-error bound. Full phase-space and Galactic
covariance, host response, strong encounters, physical impact-parameter
cutoffs and merger-conditioned launches remain uncalibrated.
No remnant trajectories, residence distributions or diffusion-versus-resolved
encounter comparison have been computed. The comparison locates environments
and radial regions where those physical tests would be informative.

Theoretical framing
The abstract, introduction, hypothesis section and discussion share the
residence-accuracy question. Adopted host families are stated as calculation
assumptions. The finite-encounter measure and its matched diffusion moments,
backward occupation equation and stopped-generator residual are formulated
analytically. The diffusion-based first response is a proposed predictor,
not an exact full-strength result. Finite-population feedback and evolving
hosts are separate controlled comparisons. Numerical result values are
unchanged; no new encounter or residence calculation was performed.
